The introduction page Explosive bow step Preparations Put in some fuses in the pillars and play simeon says Use the trap to bring the rod and a character down. Put the rod in the slot and fill the square stone tablets The final step and Boss Fight

After this part you will have to memorize 3 symbols from top to bottom. With those symbols you well have to put it in order at the computer, but this only works if the trap next to the pillars was activated at least once. You well also need to collect the stone piece (can only be done by going back in a 2nd time), Blue Celender and fuses. You well then be where the rocket is at and see that a panzer well spawn that you can kill.
